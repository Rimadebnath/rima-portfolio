About

a little more about me

born and raised in a defence family, i moved across the country more times than i can count. this taught me adaptability, resilience, and the art of making a home anywhere.

i have soft spots for fountain pens, used books (the best ones come with annotations + bookmarks), black coffee, and all things structured.

outside of work, you can usually find me attempting to build lego models that are too complex for my patience, or hiking through misty trails.

my transition from engineering to product management was fueled by a desire to bridge the gap between technical complexity and user simplicity. i love the "why" as much as the "how".
